# Puts with two exercise dates per day: default label check

Not part of the paper. `experiments/tournament.py --part tpd_check / tpd_check_ref / tpd_random / tpd_random_ref`, `experiments/two_per_day_report.py`.

**Label.** `MultiConfig(vf_time=0.02)`: the default label of the `carlos` branch (one (S, σ) run of 300,000 main + 100,000 pilot paths; Rho, Phi, ∂Δ/∂r, ∂Δ/∂d, Theta and Charm pathwise on its paths; pilot width of S min(0.25, 1.25 σ√T) S0; averaged out-of-sample and in-sample labels, Volga, Vanna, ∂Δ/∂r, ∂Δ/∂d out of sample) with **504 exercise dates a year** (two per business day, J = round(504 T)) and the value-function label at the date nearest 1/50 year (as validated at daily dates in `results/american_daily/`). Theta and Charm are pathwise with the number of dates fixed; the reference's are calendar ones (the gap halves each time the dates double). `theta_pde` is the PDE-identity Theta.

**Options.** 35 put check options (the production check's: T 0.5/1/2 × σ 10/20/40 % × K 36/40/44 at r = 6 %, d = 0, plus r = d = 0, r = d = 6 %, r = d = 1 %) and 40 options from the input ranges drawn by `generate_labels.sample_options(n=40, S0=40, seed=504)` over the `carlos` ranges (S0/K 0.8–1.2, σ plateau 5–50 % with tails to 1 % and 60 %, r and d 0–5 %, T 2 months–2 years); 100 labels each, seeds `job_seed(8020 / 8021, {id}, rep)`.

**References.** Crank–Nicolson PDE: *American* = exercise at every time step, Richardson-extrapolated in the step (continuous exercise, the target); *Bermudan 504/yr* = exercise on the labels' dates.
