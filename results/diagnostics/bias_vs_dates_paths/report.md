# Bias against the number of exercise dates a year and of main paths

In-sample label (one (S, sigma) run, pathwise Rho, Phi, Theta, value-function label at 1/50 year, exercise_bound=False), puts. 15 options across the input ranges x 30 labels at every combination of 126, 252, 504, 1008 exercise dates a year and 75k, 150k, 300k, 600k main paths; pilot 100k paths, the same seeds at every combination; 7200 labels. References: American PDE and Bermudan PDE at each number of dates a year. Generated by `experiments/diagnostics/freq_paths_report.py` from the data in `raw/` (`experiments/diagnostics/freq_paths_grid.py`; SCC job scripts `scc/freq_paths*.qsub`).

## Columns

Errors in % of |American PDE|, means over the options (an option is left out of a Greek when its reference is below 1 % of the largest or its label is exact):

* **Time/label**: mean wall time of one label (s, one core, BU SCC nodes).
* **Total bias**: |mean of the labels − American PDE|.
* **Simulation bias**: |mean of the labels − Bermudan PDE at the same dates a year|: the Monte Carlo / LSM part (exercise rule fitted on the paths, regression basis, dispersed-start regression, pathwise approximation).
* **Bias from Bermudan approximation**: |Bermudan PDE − American PDE|: exercise allowed on the grid dates only; exact (no noise), halves each time the dates double.
* **Noise floor (false bias)**: √(2/π) × se, se = sd of one label / √(labels): the |bias| an unbiased label would show from the noise left in the mean of the labels. A bias close to it is not detectable; more labels lower it like 1/√labels.

The total is not the sum of the two parts: they can partly cancel within an option.
