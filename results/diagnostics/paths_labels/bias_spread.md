# Spread and confidence intervals of the per-option bias (504 dates a year)

Labels of `results/two_per_day` (300k main paths, 100 labels per option) against the American PDE; IS = in-sample, OOS = out-of-sample, Hyb = default (hybrid) label. Per option b = (mean of 100 labels − PDE) / |PDE| in %, over the options the report uses (|PDE| ≥ 1 % of the largest, non-exact label; n options per cell in the last table). Generated by `bias_spread.py`.

* **MAE [95 % CI]**: mean |b| over the options (Table 1 of the summary); CI by bootstrap over the options (4000 resamples), so it covers the label noise and the choice of options.
* **mean ± half-width**: mean signed b with a 95 % t-interval (direction of the bias).
* **SD**: standard deviation of b over the options (variance = SD²); it includes the leftover noise of each 100-label mean.
* **per-option ±**: typical 95 % half-width of one option's own bias, 1.96 × median se.
* **|t| > 3**: options whose bias is more than 3 standard errors from 0 (about 0 expected from noise alone).
